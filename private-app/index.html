How to read this. Green records have express beach-use grants; teal records have identified benefits by reference or common-use language with the grantee. Orange shows reservations for others/public. Purple means the reviewed instrument did not establish a specific benefit, not that rights are absent. Gray identifies a different subdivision or scope; gold means a parcel-limited clause has not been assigned to these polygons. Red records have an unresolved scan/reference or no supplied reference.

The audit covers 186 supplied references, with shared instruments deduplicated. A confirmed assessor typo is documented rather than silently corrected. A clause for one lot in a multi-parcel deed is not automatically extended to all lots. FEE and TAX polygons can overlap. Assessor records, historical lots, deed instruments and owners are different counting units. The property-history browser includes reviewed historical evidence and remaining research gaps. Full lifetime title chains, later instruments, enforceability and a survey-accurate plan overlay are not yet certified.
