This is a full inventory audit, not a complete title examination. The supplied references are assessor-linked instruments, not all historical deeds or necessarily the latest conveyances. Some are judgments, decrees or other non-deed records. “Checked” means the specific reviewed instrument did not establish a beach benefit; it does not mean no rights exist. FEE and TAX assessment polygons can overlap, and neither mapped-record totals nor deed totals are counts of owners or enforceable-rights holders.

This audit does not establish current enforceability, continuous title, releases, extinguishment, later transfers, association membership, road fee ownership or a survey-accurate historical-plan overlay. Public/common-use wording is retained rather than rewritten as exclusive association rights. A location or beach boundary alone is not an affirmative grant.

Unresolved scan/reference cases require Registry assistance, corrected assessor references or certified copies before they can be called complete. The audit keeps those gaps visible rather than using a loading image, repeated opening page, wrong-property document or generic clause as proof of absence.
