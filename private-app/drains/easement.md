# Hamilton Beach: The 1961 Worrall Avenue Drainage Easement

The original recorded easement and its attached survey plan establish a Town drainage corridor west of the western end of Worrall Avenue, extending toward a creek. They do not, by themselves, establish which modern outfall uses that corridor or whether the proposed pipe was built exactly as drawn. ([Plymouth County Registry of Deeds, Book 2838, Page 142 and attached plan](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))

## The recorded instrument

- **Registry reference:** Book 2838, Page 142, Plymouth County Registry of Deeds. ([Registry](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))
- **Grantor and grantee:** Bertram E. Besse granted rights to the Inhabitants of the Town of Wareham, a municipal corporation. ([Registry, 2838/142](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))
- **Dates:** Signed and acknowledged March 15, 1961; recorded March 20, 1961, at 9:00 a.m. ([Registry, 2838/142](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))
- **Location:** Besse’s premises “located Westerly of the Westerly end of Worrall Avenue at Hamilton Beach in said Wareham.” ([Registry, 2838/142](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))
- **Drainage and access rights:** The Town may enter, excavate a ditch “not more than ten feet in width” to lay drainage pipes, and pass and repass for trench digging, pipe installation, pipe repair, and necessary related work. ([Registry, 2838/142](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))
- **Restoration provision:** The Town “shall restore said property to a condition as nearly as it existed before said excavations were made.” ([Registry, 2838/142](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))
- **Duration stated:** The grant runs to the Town and its successors and assigns “forever.” ([Registry, 2838/142](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))

The excavation-width limit is express, as are installation, repair, and access rights. The instrument does not expressly promise a recurring maintenance schedule, identify a modern facility number, or establish Town ownership of every drain nearby. ([Registry, 2838/142](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))

## Where the attached plan places the route

The instrument incorporates a plan titled “Plan To Accompany Drainage Easement over land of Bertram E. Besse, Wareham, Mass.,” prepared by Walter E. Rowley & Associates, dated March 2, 1961, at a scale of one inch to forty feet. The attached sheet is marked “’61-143” and stamped “ORIGINAL PLAN ON FILE,” filed March 20, 1961. ([Registry, plan attached to 2838/142](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))

Read the route from the street toward the outlet:

1. **Street-side origin:** The plan shows Worrall Avenue approaching from Pilgrim Avenue and reaching the western end of the developed street frontage. ([Registry, attached plan](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))
2. **Across Besse’s land:** A line labeled “CL of proposed pipe” continues westward from the end of Worrall across land labeled Bertram E. Besse. A dimension of 218 feet appears along the proposed route, and 5.00-foot offsets appear on each side of its centerline. ([Registry, attached plan](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))
3. **Creek-side terminus:** The proposed pipe corridor reaches the feature labeled “Creek”; the Wareham River is separately labeled along the lower shoreline. ([Registry, attached plan](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))

Useful historical landmarks include the Rosalie A. and Muriel A. Dumais parcel beside the route, Lot 17A on the opposite street frontage, Lots 16A–16C below Worrall, and a “proposed basin” farther along Worrall toward Pilgrim Avenue. These are historical plan labels, not independently verified present-day parcel boundaries or street numbers. ([Registry, attached plan](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))

The drawing expressly labels the pipe as proposed. Treat it as the documented easement alignment, not an as-built confirmation of the pipe’s actual installation or present condition. ([Registry, attached plan](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))

## Connection to the modern outfalls

- **WWA1019PI:** The Town’s 2019 report places this outfall at the “west end of Worral Ave into creek,” which makes it the strongest geographic lead for comparison with the 1961 easement. That location description is not proof that the modern pipe occupies the recorded corridor. ([Wareham stormwater report](https://www.wareham.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1956/Stormwater-Final-Report-PDF))
- **Current WWA1019PI mapping:** The modern inventory identifies a private, eight-inch concrete outfall, and its mapped shore-directed alignment should be reconciled with the historical creek-directed easement rather than assumed identical. ([Stormwater inventory, WWA1019PI](https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/96b1dff06e7c4f378c17dc40acdaa20a/#data_s=id%3Aa4d5d6a525fe4e7f9947dd54d0f33918-StormwaterAGOL_V4_gdb_7474%3A2060))
- **WWA1349PI:** The 2019 report locates this outfall on Worrall opposite Turner Avenue, rather than at Worrall’s western end. The 1961 easement does not establish a connection to this second outlet. ([Wareham stormwater report](https://www.wareham.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1956/Stormwater-Final-Report-PDF); [Registry, 2838/142 and plan](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))

The private-owner field in the modern inventory and the Town’s historical easement rights describe different things. Neither alone settles present ownership, maintenance responsibility, or whether the modern facility uses this easement. ([Stormwater inventory, WWA1019PI](https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/96b1dff06e7c4f378c17dc40acdaa20a/#data_s=id%3Aa4d5d6a525fe4e7f9947dd54d0f33918-StormwaterAGOL_V4_gdb_7474%3A2060); [Registry, 2838/142](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))

## Later deed corroboration

A later deed, Book 53025, Page 85, expressly says the conveyed property is subject to the drainage easement in favor of the Town dated March 15, 1961, recorded in Book 2838, Page 142. This corroborates the recorded reference, but does not independently prove the present physical route, present legal enforceability, or applicability to every parcel in that multi-parcel deed. ([Registry, 53025/85](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))

## Recommended next records

I recommend requesting the following from Wareham Municipal Maintenance or the stormwater program, supplying both the easement reference and the two facility IDs:

> Please provide existing records concerning the drainage easement granted by Bertram E. Besse to the Town of Wareham, dated March 15, 1961, recorded at Plymouth County Registry of Deeds Book 2838, Page 142, with attached plan marked 1961-143, for drainage west of the western end of Worrall Avenue at Hamilton Beach.
>
> Please include construction and as-built plans; current GIS drainage and easement layers; records identifying connected catch basins and the discharge point; inspection, repair, replacement, and maintenance records; and any records relating this easement to outfalls WWA1019PI or WWA1349PI.
>
> Please also provide records supporting the “Private” ownership classification for these outfalls, including any ownership or maintenance agreements, and any later amendments, relocations, releases, or replacement easements.

Wareham’s department-contact page lists Municipal Maintenance at 508-295-5300, 95 Charge Pond Road; the Town also maintains a stormwater information page. ([Town department contacts](https://www.wareham.ma.us/department-contacts); [Town stormwater page](https://www.wareham.gov/289/Stormwater))
