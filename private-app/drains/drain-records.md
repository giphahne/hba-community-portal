# Hamilton Beach Drainage Records: Additional Findings

This follow-up found a third documented Hamilton Beach outfall, WWA1030PI, near the beach end of Worrall Avenue and Reynolds Avenue, plus its associated catch-basin sample from September 2022. A newer collection also provides mapped catchment sheets and laboratory tables for the two previously identified Worrall Avenue outfalls. ([Wareham outfall reports, PDF pages 87–88, 95–96 and 153–154](https://stormwater.buzzardsbay.org/documents/Wareham-Outfall-Reports.pdf))

## Nearby drainage systems: keep separate from Hamilton Beach’s outlets

The same collection includes **WWA1022PI** and **WWA1024PI**, both labeled Broadmarsh Avenue and discharging to a tributary of Broad Marsh River, MA95-49. They should not be conflated with the three Worrall Avenue outlets discharging toward the Wareham River/beach. ([Wareham outfall reports, PDF pages 89 and 91](https://stormwater.buzzardsbay.org/documents/Wareham-Outfall-Reports.pdf))

Both sheets describe an underground infiltration practice and recommend inspecting it during rain, cleaning as needed, continuing monitoring, and checking for potential illicit discharge. WWA1024PI is identified as a circular 12-inch corrugated-metal pipe. These are recommendations, not records confirming that cleaning or repairs were performed. ([Wareham outfall reports, PDF pages 89 and 91](https://stormwater.buzzardsbay.org/documents/Wareham-Outfall-Reports.pdf))

The separate IDDE report groups WWA1024PI and WWA1022PI with Broadmarsh Avenue, Pilgrim Avenue, Shore Avenue, and Sherman Street, stating “No issues found.” Its heading is dated November 2, 2022, but its field-work narrative includes 2023 dates; that internal inconsistency prevents treating the heading as a reliable date for all inspections. ([Wareham IDDE report](https://stormwater.buzzardsbay.org/documents/Wareham-IDDE-Report.pdf))

## Limits and records still needed

I did not locate a second executed Hamilton Beach drainage easement, an as-built confirming the 1961 Besse easement pipe, or a Hamilton Beach-specific cleaning/repair work order in the sources reviewed during this pass. This was not an exhaustive Registry grantor/grantee search or a search of nonpublic Town files.

The Town’s “Stormwater Analytics” PDF is website-traffic analytics for August–September 2019, not drainage test results. The FY23 summary is a townwide program summary, not a Hamilton Beach inspection sheet; the posted SSO PDF yielded no extractable text, so no location-specific finding was drawn from it. ([Town analytics PDF](https://www.wareham.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1959/Stormwater-Analytics-PDF); [FY23 summary](https://stormwater.buzzardsbay.org/documents/Wareham-Summary-FY23.pdf); [posted SSO document](https://www.wareham.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1958/Stormwater-SSO-Data-PDF))

### Expanded records-request identifiers

For a request to the Town or the Buzzards Bay Stormwater Collaborative, I recommend including:

- **Outfalls:** WWA1019PI, WWA1349PI, and newly identified WWA1030PI.
- **Catch basins:** WWA1157CB, WWA1184CB, WWA1210CB, and map-labeled WWA1191CB.
- **Historic legal record:** Book 2838, Page 142 and attached plan 1961-143.
- **Requested records:** As-builts, pipe-connection diagrams, catch-basin inspection/cleaning logs, CCTV footage and reports, repair/replacement work orders, original laboratory certificates and units, later sampling results, and documents supporting private-owner classifications.

The identifiers above come from the catchment reports and the recorded easement; the recommended document types are investigative next steps, not a claim that every such record exists. ([Wareham outfall reports](https://stormwater.buzzardsbay.org/documents/Wareham-Outfall-Reports.pdf); [Plymouth Registry, 2838/142](https://titleview.org/plymouthdeeds/))
