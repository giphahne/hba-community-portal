# Hamilton Beach: Earlier Deed Tracing for Purple Parcels

Research checkpoint: October 11, 2026. This ledger covers the original 100 purple map records in 97 deed groups, including shared instruments and vacant parcels. It is a bounded research pass, not a title certification or a determination of enforceable rights.

## Reading this ledger

- **Positive evidence:** A specific earlier clause may support a parcel benefit only after historical lot and chain continuity are verified. Map changes require a separate parent adjudication.
- **Partial:** One or more earlier instruments were examined, but the chain is not complete. No-clause findings concern only the instrument read.
- **Blocked:** A specific scan, reference, or name-search obstacle remains. Capture and index metadata alone are not a substantive deed review.
- **Page counts:** Earlier-instrument page counts are research checkpoint fields. The instrument notes identify unreadable pages, continuation problems, and unresolved limits.
